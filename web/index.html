The Open LLM Architecture Lineage

103 open-weight models (83 once sizes of one release are folded together) from Sebastian Raschka's LLM Architecture Gallery, linked to their closest architectural ancestors by config.json similarity and laid out by generation. Hover a node for its architecture, click it to trace its lineage. Method and data: github.com/wenhaochai/llm-architecture-lineage.
